Our Technology

Engineered for precision and computational reproducibility in computational pathology.

Nuclei Segmentation

Powered by a fully convolutional U-Net architecture with skip connections to delineate pixel-accurate nuclear boundaries across H&E stained colorectal tissue slides.

Phenotype Classification

A multi-class CNN classifier evaluates 32×32 nuclear crops to identify five distinct phenotypes: Malignant/Dysplastic, Inflammatory, Healthy Epithelial, Stromal, and Other.

Quantitative Metrics

Automated watershed instance separation calculates exact nuclear counts, relative population ratios, and density distribution for reproducible quantitative evaluation.
